The countdown, decision and discard effects are FLAC encodings of the WAV files at
commit 9670029a2dae985cfff6de7f28db17276ab8c0c9, in this directory.

Encoding command, once per source:
  ffmpeg -i source.wav -map_metadata -1 -codec:a flac -compression_level 12 \
    -metadata_header_padding 0 output.flac

The countdown's floating-point samples become 24-bit PCM; the other effects retain
their 16-bit PCM samples. Sample rates and channel counts are unchanged.

Use FLAC for these short effects: a 64 kbps MP3 trial added about 25 ms of leading
decoder padding in WebKit. Chromium and WebKit decode the FLAC files with the
original sample counts and onset timing. Audio is still loaded eagerly.
